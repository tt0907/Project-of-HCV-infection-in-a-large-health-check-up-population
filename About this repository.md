This repository contains the analysis code and aggregated data to reproduce the key findings of the study: “Prevalence and incidence of hepatitis C virus infection in a large health check-up population in China: a cross-sectional and longitudinal study of 1.6 million adults” (Sailimai et al., GigaScience 2026).
The project aims to estimate the prevalence of anti-HCV and the incidence rate of HCV seroconversion between 2017 and 2023, along with detailed analyses across different geographical regions and subpopulations. By sharing the R scripts and data files, we aim to promote open science and ensure the reproducibility of our findings.
